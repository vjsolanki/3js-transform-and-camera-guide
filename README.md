A small interactive UI to help understand how Three.js transforms (position, rotation, scale) and the `PerspectiveCamera` work.

## What you'll see

- **View 1 — Camera View**: what the Three.js camera renders.
- **View 2 — Scene View**: you, floating outside, watching the same scene. Drag to orbit. The yellow pyramid is the camera; the dashed yellow line is its `lookAt`.
- **Sidebar**: a 7-step guided lesson, plus sliders for every cube and camera property.

## How to use it

1. Open the page.
2. Follow Step 1 → Step 7 in the **Lesson** panel on the right.
3. Each step tells you exactly which slider to drag and what to watch.
4. After Step 7, play with the presets and combine sliders freely.
