2 · Scene View — drag to orbit
You floating outside the scene. The little camera icon + yellow pyramid is the same camera that's drawing view #1. Drag to look around.

Legend — what you're looking at

The cube is your Object3D. It has a transform: position, rotation, scale. Faces are color-coded so you can see rotation.
Red line is the world X axis (right).
Green line is the world Y axis (up).
Blue line is the world Z axis (toward you).
Yellow pyramid (view #2 only) = camera frustum. Anything inside this pyramid ends up in view #1.
Yellow dashed line = sight line from camera to its lookAt target. The yellow dot is the target.

Presets

Cube · position

Where the cube lives. Origin (0,0,0) is where the colored axes meet.

Cube · rotation (radians)

Tilt. π=half turn, 2π=full turn.

Cube · scale

1=normal. Non-uniform = squashed.

Camera · position

Where the camera stands. Watch the yellow pyramid move in view #2.

Camera · FOV (degrees)

Lens angle. Low=telephoto/zoomed in. High=fisheye/wide.

Camera · lookAt target

Where the camera aims (the yellow dot in view #2).
